## צְבָא (id: BDB11321)

\[Str6634,6633] † \[צְבָא] **vb.** be inclined, desire, be pleased (Targum Syr.; cf. BH II. צבה);—**Pe. 1\.** *desire:* *Pf.* 1 S. צְבִית (K§ 47 Beisp. 1\) *a)* NöGGA, 1884, 1019 pf. intrans.) Dn 7:19 (c. inf.). **2\.** *be pleased*, *will* (without hindrance), abs., of God: *Impf.* 3 ms. יִצְבֵּא 4:14, 4:22, 4:29; *Inf.* sf. מִצְבְּיֵהּ v:32 acc. to *his willing;* of Neb.: *Pt. act.* צָבֵא 5:19, 5:19, 5:19, 5:19

> \[Str6640] † צְבוּ **n.f. thing, anything** (orig. *purpose*) (Palm. צבו Lzb357 SAC100 Cooke266, Syr. ܨܒܘܬܐ, all *thing;* cf. NH חֵפֶץ *thing*, from הָפֵץ *delight in, crave*);—Dn 6:18.

* **Associated Passages:** Daniel 4:29; Daniel 5:19; Daniel 7:19; Daniel 6:18; Daniel 4:22; Daniel 4:32; Daniel 4:14


## I צְבַע (id: BDB11323)

\[Str6647,6648] I. \[צְבַע] **vb. dip, wet** (Targum Syr.; cf. BH I. צבע);—**Pa.** *Pt. act.* pl. מְצַבְּעִין Dn 4:22 *wet* thee (ל), \+ מִטַּל. **Hithpa.** *Impf.* יִצְטַבּע (also בַָּע\-), *be wet*, c. בְּטַל 4:12, 4:20, מִטַּל v:30, 5:21.

* **Associated Passages:** Daniel 5:21; Daniel 4:20; Daniel 4:22; Daniel 4:30; Daniel 4:12


## II צבע (id: BDB11324)

> \[Str6655,6654] † צַא **n.\[m.]** side (very rare in Aram.; perh. Hebr., v. BH *id.,* √ צדד);—cstr., c. prep.: ´לְח: עליא Dn 7:25, i.e. *against;* מַלְכוּתָא ´מִצּ: 6:5, i.e. *arising from, touching*.

> \[Str6665,6663,6666] † צִדְקָה **n.f. right doing** (Talm., OAram.; v. BH צְדָקָה, √ צדק);—´צ: Dn 4:24.

## צְלָא (id: BDB11330)

\[Str6739] † \[צְלָא] **vb. Pa.** pray (orig. *bow* in prayer) (Targum *id., bow,* Pa, *pray,* so Syr. ܨܠܐ and Pa.; As. *sullû, entreat* (appar. not of prayer to gods ZimKAT 3\. 610 f.); Ar. صَلَنا *middle of the back*, صلا II. *pray*, Eth. ፀለወ፡ *bow*, ፀለየ፡ *pray;* Sab. צלות *shrine* HomChr125; AA 185\);—*Pt.* מְצַלֵּא Dn 6:11 (abs.); pl. מְצַלַּ֫יִן (K§ 47a)) Ezr 6:10 (c. ל *in behalf of*).

* **Associated Passages:** Daniel 6:11; Ezra 6:10


## צְלַח (id: BDB11331)
