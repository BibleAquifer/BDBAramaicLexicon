## טְאֵב (id: BDB10997)

\[Str2868,2895,888] † טְאֵב **vb.** be good (Targum Syr., v. BH טוֺב);—**Pe** *Pf.* 3 ms. ´ט:, c. עַל Pers. [Dn 6:24](https://ref.ly/Dan6:24) *it was good to him \= he was glad* (cf. בְּאֵשׁ v:[15](https://ref.ly/Dan6:15)). p. 1084

> \[Str2869,2896] † טָב **adj.** good;—abs. ´ט: [Dn 2:32](https://ref.ly/Dan2:32) *pure* gold; \= *pleasing* to (עַל Pers.) [Ezr 5:17](https://ref.ly/Ezra5:17) (cf. [6:18](https://ref.ly/Ezra6:18), BH טוֺב **5**).

> \[Str2876–77] † \[טַבָּח] **n.m.** guardsman (v. BH *id.;* √ טבח);—pl. emph. טַבָּחַיָא [Dn 2:14](https://ref.ly/Dan2:14).

> \[Str2906] † טוּר **n.m.** mountain (Targum Syr. Nab., SAC58; BH צוּר, √ V. צור);—abs. ´ט: [Dn 2:35](https://ref.ly/Dan2:35); emph. טוּרָא v:[45](https://ref.ly/Dan2:45).

> \[Str2908] † טְוָת **adv.** fastingly, hungrily (Syr. طوْت *id.;* PS1465; on etym. cf. SchulthHom. Wurz. 32 f.; on adv. force of f. term. תָ֯, v. Nö§ 155 A, M 201 WCG 135; and cf. רַבַּת [Psalm 124:3](https://ref.ly/Ps124:3));—[Dn 6:18](https://ref.ly/Dan6:18).

> \[Str2917] † \[טִין] **n.\[m.]** clay (Targum *id.;* NH טִינָה, Syr. ܛܝܢܐ, Ar. ܛܦܪܐ (yet v. Frä8));—emph. חֲסַף טִינָא [Dn 2:41](https://ref.ly/Dan2:41), [2:43](https://ref.ly/Dan2:43).

> \[Str2919–20] † טַל **n.\[m.]** dew (v. BH I. טלל);—cstr. טַל שְׁמַיָא [Dn 4:12](https://ref.ly/Dan4:12), [4:20](https://ref.ly/Dan4:20), [4:22](https://ref.ly/Dan4:22), [4:30](https://ref.ly/Dan4:30), [5:31](https://ref.ly/Dan5:31).

> \[Str2927,2926,6751] † \[טְלַל] **vb. Haph.** have shade (Targum Syr.; v. BH III. \[צָלַל]);—*Impf.* 3 fs. תַּטְלֵל [Dn 4:9](https://ref.ly/Dan4:9) (beasts under tree).

> \[Str2939,2938] † \[טְעֵם] **vb. Pa.** feed (Pe. prop. *taste,* v. BH);—*Impf.* 3 mpl., acc. of grass \+ ל Pers.: יְמַעְָמוּן [Dn 4:22](https://ref.ly/Dan4:22), [4:29](https://ref.ly/Dan4:29), so (sf. Pers.) יְטַעֲמוּנֵהּ [5:21](https://ref.ly/Dan5:21).

* **Associated Passages:** Daniel 4:29; Ezra 5:17; Daniel 6:24; Daniel 2:32; Daniel 4:20; Daniel 5:31; Daniel 4:22; Daniel 2:45; Psalms 124:3; Daniel 2:43; Daniel 6:15; Daniel 4:9; Daniel 2:14; Daniel 6:18; Daniel 4:30; Daniel 5:21; Daniel 2:35; Daniel 2:41; Ezra 6:18; Daniel 4:12


## טְרַד (id: BDB11008)

[Dn 4:22](https://ref.ly/Dan4:22), [4:29](https://ref.ly/Dan4:29). **Pe‘îl** *Pf.* 3 ms. טְרִיד (WCG 224\), subj. Pers. \+ מִן [4:30](https://ref.ly/Dan4:30), [5:21](https://ref.ly/Dan5:21).

* **Associated Passages:** Daniel 5:21; Daniel 4:29; Daniel 4:22; Daniel 4:30; Ezra 4:9
