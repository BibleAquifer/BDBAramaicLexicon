## גאה (id: BDB10828)

> \[Str1344,1467] † גֵּוָה **n. f.** pride (Targum גֵּיוָה; BH גֵּאָה; cf. K§ 55, l (but also 6 a));—abs. ´בְּג: Dn 4:34 *in pride.*

> \[Str1355,1354] † \[גַּב] **n. \[m.]** either back (Targum גַּב *back, top;* BH גַּב, √ גנב), or \< side (√ גנב, cf. Ar. جَنْبٌ side, Syr. ܓܒܐ *side*, √ ܓܢܒ;—v. also DWB 65\);—pl. sf. Dn 7:6 Kt wings עַל־גַּבַּיהּ on *its sides* (Bev Behrm Dr; Qr גַּבַּהּ perh. *its back,* so most).

> \[Str1358,1359] † \[גֹּב] **n. m.** pit, den of lions (Targum גּוּב, Syr. ܓܘܒ *pit;*? cf. BH גֹּב n.pr.loc., √ גבב);—cstr. ´ג: Dn 6:8, 6:25; גּוֺב v:13; emph. גֻּבָּא v:17, v:18, v:20, v:21, v:24, v:24, v:25.
