## דִּי (id: BDB10876)

\[Str1768,2088,834] דִּי **part of relation**, who, which, that, used also as **mark of gen.** and **conj.** that, because (Aram. of Nineveh, Bab. Zinj. Nerab, Cilicia, Têma, Egypt, זִי (Lzb267. 446 RES361 S\-CPap. A 2 \+); Nab. Palm. די; Targum דְּ (exc. in cpds., as דִּילִי *mine,* דִּילֵיהּ *his*); Sam. ࠃ; Syr. ܕ; Eth. ዝ፡ *za:* of same origin as Ar. دُو *possessor of* \[cf. sub BH זֶה]. Properly a demonstr. *that* \[cf. זֶה; in Eth. *ze* is ‘this’, *za* ‘which’]; but this being referred by usage to something preceding becomes equiv. to the relative *who, which,* used, however, more widely than Heb. אֲשֶׁר);—**1\.** as **rel.** *who, which* (construed like אֲשֶׁר): a. Je 10:11, Dn 2:11 דִּי מְדָֽרְהוֺן *whose* dwelling, v:24 דִּי מַנִּי מַלְבָּא *whom* the king had appointed, v:26 ´דִּי שְׁמֵהּ בּ: *whose* name was B., 4:5, 5:12, 5:23, etc.; \= *that which* 2:23; \= *him that* Ezr 7:25; 6:15, Dn 7:17 דִּת אִנוּן אַרְבַּע *which* are four (v. אִנּוּן). Sq. pron. of 2 ps. (cf. אֲשֶׁר **3**), Dn 2:37 thou, O king ..., יְהַב לָח…דִּי: *to whom*... hath given, 4:19, 4:6 *as to whom* I know, etc. (cf. אֲשֶׁר **4 d** *end*). Sq. תַּמָּה \= *where* Ezr 6:1, so דִּי alone 6:3, Dn 2:38; of time, בְּעִדָּנָא דִּי at the time *when,* etc., 3:5, 3:15. With the pred. an inf. c. לְ, Dn 6:9 כְּתָבָא דִּי לָא לְהַשְׁנָיָה *which* is not to be changed, Ezr 6:8; a place\- or other determination, Dn 3:20 valiant men בְחֵילֵהּ *that* were in his army, 5:2 הֵיכְלָא דִּי בִירוּשְׁלֵב, 7:20, Ezr 4:24, 5:6, 6:2, 6:6; cf. Dn 2:25, 5:13, 7:7, Ezr 7:23. דִּי לָא \= *without,* Ezr 6:9, 7:22 (so דְּלָא Targum Gn 15:2, Ex 21:11); cf. Dn 2:34, 2:45. **b.** מָן דִּי, מָה דִּי (cf. in late Heb. ´מַה־שֶּׁ:, מַה **1 e** *b*) *whoever, whatever,* so † Dn 3:6, 3:11 יִפֵּל מָֽן־דִּי־לָא *whoever* does not fall down, 4:14 לְמָן דִּי יִצְבֵּא to *whomsoever* he willeth, v:22, v:29, 5:21; מָה דִי *whatever* (or simply *what*), † Dn 2:28 מָה דִּי לֶהֱוֵא what will be, v:29, v:29, v:45, Ezr 6:8, 7:18.—Cf. K§ 103\. **2\.** as **mark of the gen.,** Dn 2:15 שׁליטא די מלכא prop. the captain, *that of* the king \= *the king’s captain* (a genuine Aram. idiom: so Targum דְּ, Syr. ܕ constantly), v:19, v:25, v:49 \+ oft.: the subst. in such cases may be either in the emph. state (determined), as ll. cc. Ezr 4:15, 5:2, etc., or in the abs. state (undetermined), Dn 5:5, 7:4, 7:9, 7:10 נְהַר דִּי נוּר; or it may have a pleon. sf., 2:20 שְׁמֵהּ דִּי אֱלָהָא lit. his name, *that of* God \= *God’s name*, v:44, 3:8, 3:25, 3:26, 4:23, etc. (so also Targum Syr.). To circumscribe an adj., esp. in specif. of the *material:* Dn 2:38 thou art רֵאשָׁה דִּי דַהֲבָא the head of gold, v:39, 3:1, 5:7, 5:16, Ezr 5:14, 6:4 \+; as predic. Dn 2:32 רֵאשֵׁהּ דִּי־דְהַב טָב his head (was) *of* fine gold, v:33, 7:19; with a pron. Dn 2:20 wisdom and might דִּי־לֵהּ הִיא are *his;* cf. 6:27 וּמַלְכוּתֵהּ דִּי־לָא תִתְחַבַּל his kingdom (is one) which shall not be destroyed, 7:14.—Vid. further K§ 81\. **3\.** as **conj.** (cf. אֲשֶׁר **8**): **a.** *that (quod)*, after vbs. of knowing, Dn 2:8, 2:9, seeing 2:45, 3:27, hearing 5:14, etc.; introducing the subject of a sentence, …דִּי:…יְדִיעַ: 3:18, Ezr 4:13; Dn 2:47 …מִן־קְשֹׁט דִּי: True is it *that …*  (cf. אָמְנָם כִּי Jb 12:2). **b.** \= *in that, inasmuch as, whereas:* Dn 2:41 and *whereas* (דִּי) thou sawest, etc. … it shall be a divided kingdom, v:43, 4:20, 4:23; as a connecting link \= *seeing that, because, for* (cf. אֲשֶׁר **8 c**) 2:9, 2:20 b, 2:23 b, 2:47 b, 4:15, 6:24 b. **c.** *that (ut)*, after vbs. of asking Dn 2:16, commanding 3:10, 3:29, expressing a purpose 4:3, 5:15, Ezr 4:15, 6:10 \+; דִּי לָא *that not (ne)* Dn 2:18, 3:28, 6:18 (on דִּי לְמָה *lest* Ezr 7:23, see מה). **d.** prefixed to direct narr. (like כִּי **1 b,** and sts. אֲשֶׁר **8 a** γ, and ὅτι *recitativum*), Dn 2:25 and said thus unto him דִּי־הַשְׁכַּחַת (that) I have found, etc., 5:7, 6:6, 6:14. **4\.** with preps. and other prefixes: **a.** כְּדִי (like Heb. כַּאֲשֶׁר; so Eg. Aram. כזי, Palm. Nab. כדי, Lzb293 SACG1 62, 63 Cooke369 b RES361; Targum כַּד, Syr. ܟܕ); *a according as*, Dn 2:43; *b. so soon as, when*, 3:7, 5:20, 6:11, 6:15. **b.** מִן־דִּי: *a. because that*, Dn 3:22, Ezr 5:12 (cf. מֵאֲשֶׁר Is 43:4); *b. from* (the time) *that, after (ex quo)*, Dn 4:23, Ezr 4:23. **c.** עַד דִּי *until*, Dn 2:9, 2:34, 4:30, 7:22; \= *ere that* 6:25. **d.** עַל דִּי Dn 3:19 is not a conj., but means *above that which ...* **e.** for עַל דִּבְרַת דִּי and כָּל־קֳבֵל דִּי see \[דִּבְרָה] and קֳבֵל.
